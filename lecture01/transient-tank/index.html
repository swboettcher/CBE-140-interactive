From the integral conservation law to a tank balance

d/dt ∫CV ρ dV = − ∫CS ρ v·n dA
For a fixed control volume, the left side is accumulation. The surface integral is net outward mass flow.
↓ discrete inlet and outlet streams
dm/dt = Σ ṁin − Σ ṁout
Take-home point: the familiar tank balance is a simplified form of the general integral conservation law.

Constant-density liquid simplification

m = ρV,   ṁ = ρQ
ρ dV/dt = ρQin − ρQout
dV/dt = Qin − Qout
V = ATh   ⇒   dh/dt = (Qin − Qout)/AT
Take-home point: for an incompressible liquid of constant density, a volumetric-flow imbalance directly determines how fast the tank level changes.
